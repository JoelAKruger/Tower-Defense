Cards:

Grey:
Basic advance - Attack a cell adjacent to one of your cells
Basic defend - Reinforce one of your cells (should really be called reinforce)
Reveal - Let a cell be revealed for the rest of the game

Green:
Gamble - 20% chance to receive a red card, 20% chance to lose all your cards
Secret defence - Secretly upgrade one of your cells, getting attacked will set off a trap
Launch a boat

Gold:
Blind other players - Other players cannot see adjacent cells
Strike attack - Attack a group of 7 cells at any spot on the board
Arrow attack - Attack a line of cells originating from one of your cells

Red:
Bribe - Force another player to attack a player of your choosing
Block - Prevent another player from making a move
Steal - Take a random card from another player
